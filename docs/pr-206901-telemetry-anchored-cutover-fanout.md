# Telemetry-anchored cutover and fanout safety for cohort-aware rollouts

Problem: canary promotions and cohort fanouts sometimes proceeded before telemetry signals across cohorts reached parity, causing partial regressions and hard-to-reproduce failures. This PR introduces a telemetry-anchored cutover that gates traffic promotion on signal completeness and cohort parity, plus a fanout safety layer that quarantines shadow lanes with incomplete coverage. Motivation: reduce noisy rollouts, make automated promotes safer for heterogeneous cohorts and global regions, and provide clear observability when promotions are held. Changes include a novel TelemetryProbe that computes cohort-level completeness and distribution divergence against a rolling baseline, a CutoverCoordinator that delays commit until probes pass or an explicit manual override, and a FanoutQuarantine that isolates under-sampled shadow lanes and drains them slowly until they reach readiness. This is intended to complement existing metric gates and the rollout orchestrator, not replace them. If a rollout is stuck in CUTOVER_HELD: check 'rollout.cutover.status' traces in Console, verify cohort_coverage metric and KL_divergence in the last 10m window. To manually override, use 'redwood rollout cutover-override <rollout-id> --reason "operator-triage"'. If quarantine labels cause high cardinality, toggle rollout.quarantine.label_sampling_pct to 0.0 to disable labels temporarily and open a support ticket.
